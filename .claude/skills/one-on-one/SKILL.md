# 1:1 Prep

Argument: person name or seat (e.g. `Bri`, `ops-manager`, `Hannah`, `va`).

1. Read `team/<seat>.md` (growth goals, delegation items, last 1:1 log).
2. ClickUp (read-only): their open tasks, Rocks, and Scorecard measurables they own (lists in `CLAUDE.md`). Note anything overdue or off track.
3. Fathom: recent 1:1s with Betty plus their part in recent Ops L10s (To-Dos they own, issues they raised). Only state what was said.
4. Brief:
   - **Recognize:** 1–2 wins, each tied to a Core Value (Positive, Relentless, Ownership, Invested, Impact)
   - **Rocks and measurables:** on/off track, with what's needed
   - **Their issues:** what they may need to raise
   - **Delegate & Elevate:** the next item from `team/delegation-ladder.md`
   - **Coaching question:** one GWC-based question (Get it / Want it / Capacity)
5. If Betty shares notes after, append a dated entry to the 1:1 log in their team file.
