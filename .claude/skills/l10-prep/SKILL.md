# L10 Prep

Prepare the **Operations L10** in EOS format. **Read ClickUp and Fathom only; never change ClickUp.**

## Steps
1. **Last week's L10 (Fathom):** Find the most recent Ops L10 recording (see `eos/l10/README.md` for how to search). Read its summary or transcript and pull out the To-Dos committed, issues solved, and issues dropped down. Only state what the recording says.
2. **ClickUp** (workspace `1244243`), open tasks via `clickup_filter_tasks`:
   - Scorecard `900601602271`, Rocks `900601602270`, Headlines `900601602278`, IDS `900601602268`, To-Dos `900601602274`
   - Leadership IDS `901001218665` for items assigned to Betty
3. Create `eos/l10/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (next L10 date) from `_template.md`:
   - **Scorecard:** each measurable, owner, on/off. List off-track items under IDS.
   - **Rocks:** each Rock, owner, on/off. List off-track items under IDS.
   - **Headlines:** customer/employee headlines only. Availability notes go on a one-line "Team availability."
   - **To-Dos:** last week's (from Fathom + ClickUp), done/not done, and % complete (goal 90%+). Flag To-Dos said aloud in the meeting that never made it into ClickUp.
   - **IDS:** a proposed **top 3** in priority order, each with a one-line possible root issue to start *Identify*. Flag issues that keep coming back week after week.
4. **Coach Betty** in 3–5 bullets: EOS hygiene issues (Rocks with 2+ owners, To-Dos with no date, measurables with no goal, IDS that ran long without a Solve), and anything to cascade to the Leadership L10.
5. Update `eos/scorecard.md`, `eos/rocks/<quarter>.md`, and `eos/issues-list.md` if statuses changed.

Cite ClickUp task links and Fathom recording links.
