# Quarterly Planning

1. **Review:** `eos/rocks/<current quarter>.md` plus the ClickUp Rocks lists. Mark each done or not done, with lessons.
2. **Look back at the L10s:** scan this quarter's `eos/l10/` recaps for recurring issues, chronic off-track measurables, and To-Do completion trend.
3. **V/TO check:** `eos/vto.md` and `strategy/3-year-picture-tracker.md`. What must be true in 90 days to stay on pace?
4. **Long-term issues:** `eos/issues-list.md`, ClickUp "ideas to get after," Leadership IDS ideas. Decide which become Rocks.
5. **New Rocks:** 3–7 company Rocks plus 3–7 per person. SMART, one owner each. Create `eos/rocks/<next quarter>.md`.
6. **Scorecard:** propose adds, drops, or goal changes in `eos/scorecard.md`.
7. **People:** People Analyzer (`eos/people-analyzer.md`) and a 5-5-5 agenda per direct report: 5 Core Values, 5 roles, 5 Rocks.
8. Remind Betty to enter final Rocks in ClickUp (or offer to, if she asks).
